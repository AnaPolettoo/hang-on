Hang On

Privacy policy

Last updated July 28, 2026

Hang On doesn't collect your data. Every photo, every color, every measurement it takes stays on your phone — nothing is uploaded, sold, or shared, because there's nowhere for it to go. There are no servers behind this app.

What the app looks at

Hang On uses your phone's camera for two things: a one-time selfie to read your colorimetry (skin, hair, and eye tone), and photos of clothing you're cataloguing or considering buying.

DataWhere it's processed
Selfie for colorimetryOn-device, using Apple's Vision framework. The photo itself is discarded after the three color samples are taken; only the resulting palette is kept.
Clothing photosOn-device, using Core ML and Vision to guess category and color. Stored locally so the piece shows up in your closet.
Your nameStored locally, used only to personalize the on-screen greeting.
Stylist verdictsGenerated on-device by Apple Intelligence (Foundation Models), when available. No prompt or image leaves the phone.

All of it lives in your phone's local storage. Deleting the app deletes it.

What never happens

  • No account, sign-in, or email is required to use the app.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included.
  • No photo, color sample, or usage data is sent to a server — Hang On doesn't have one.
  • Nothing is shared with third parties, because nothing leaves the device in the first place.

Siri & Shortcuts

If you use Hang On's Siri Shortcuts ("Check a Piece," "My Palette," "Closet Summary"), the phrase you speak is processed by Siri under Apple's own privacy rules. Hang On only receives the request to run the shortcut — it answers using the same on-device data described above.

Children's privacy

Hang On isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age — the on-device design applies equally to every user.

Changes to this policy
